The public record

What has happened
so far.

01

DAY 1 — THE IDEA

Set an objectively unreasonable target.

Started with €0 and set the goal at €1 billion. The experiment officially begins.

02

DAY 1 — THE WEBSITE

Make the experiment public.

Built IWANNABERICH to track the real number, document the decisions, and make the whole thing public.

03

DAY 2 — SOCIAL MEDIA

The internet barely noticed.

Tried TikTok, Instagram and X. The first attempts produced almost no meaningful traction.

04

DAY 4 — REDDIT

Reddit became the first real signal.

The experiment reached roughly 1.8K views and generated 9 comments. Apparently, Reddit was more interested than TikTok.

05

DAY 5 — TIKTOK

Trying to figure out why nobody sees it.

Tested different formats, trends, AI-generated content and manually created content. The result stayed close to zero.

06

DAY 6 — PAID PROMOTION

Even TikTok said no.

Tried promoting the project. TikTok rejected the promotion, so organic discovery remains the challenge.

07

NOW — THE NEXT TARGET

Find the first 100 real visitors.

The goal isn't viral fame anymore. It's finding the first people who genuinely care enough to come back.
